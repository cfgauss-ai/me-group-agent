## What time does the weekly meetup happen?

Our weekly meetup happens every Thursday at 7pm (local time), at the usual
spot near the community center.
tags: meetup, schedule, events

## How do I become a member?

Fill out the sign-up form linked in the group description and wait for a
moderator to approve your request. Approval usually takes less than a day.
tags: membership, signup, rules

## Is there a membership fee?

No, this is a free community group. There are no fees or paid tiers.
